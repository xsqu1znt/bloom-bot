# Bloom

Bloom is a Discord bot for collecting K-pop cards, building your collection, and sharing the experience with the community.

Use Bloom to discover cards, manage your inventory, create albums and wishlists, earn in-bot rewards, and trade with other members of your server.

## Support

Need help or have a question? Join the [Bloom support server](https://discord.gg/playbloom).
