# Bloom Bot Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 3, 2026

**Last updated:** September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **the Bloom team** (“Bloom,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and retains information when you use the Bloom Discord bot. Bloom is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Discord Inc.

For information about how Discord handles your information, review [Discord’s Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy). Discord may process and retain messages, account information, and other data independently of Bloom.

## 1. Information Bloom processes

Bloom processes the following categories of information, depending on the features used and the permissions granted to it in a server.

### Discord and server identifiers

Bloom may receive and store your Discord user ID, the ID of a Discord server and channel where an action occurs, and information needed to identify a member or send a response. It may also store your Discord account creation date and, where available, the date you joined a server. Bloom may temporarily use your Discord username, display name, avatar, and member information to render responses, but the application database is designed around Discord IDs and does not intentionally store usernames or avatars as profile records.

### Profile and gameplay data

When you use Bloom, it may store:

- profile data such as a bio and wishlist entries;
- coins, petals, daily and weekly streaks, claim timestamps, cooldowns, and reminder settings;
- card and inventory data such as card IDs, inventory IDs, print numbers, condition, tags, gradients, gift history, claim location, and claim timestamps;
- albums and personal tag names or emojis; and
- virtual-currency and card-transfer records, including sender and recipient IDs, item or currency amounts, server and channel IDs, and transaction timestamps.

These records are used to provide the collection, profile, economy, reminder, and community features requested through Discord.

### Usage and moderation information

Bloom records usage telemetry associated with a user and server, including command names and categories, command counts, active dates, activity by UTC hour, last activity time, reward and card activity totals, and virtual-currency totals. It may also store cooldown state, blacklist or moderation records, moderation reasons, inventory IDs affected by moderation, and related timestamps.

Bloom reads message content, interaction data, and attachments when necessary to recognize commands, process a requested feature, or respond in Discord. Raw message content is not intentionally retained as a general message archive in Bloom’s application database. Content that a feature saves—such as a bio, tag, album name, or staff-managed trivia entry—is retained as described above.

Authorized staff may use Bloom to import card artwork from Discord attachments. The resulting card artwork may be stored and served through BunnyCDN or another configured content-delivery provider. Do not upload personal or confidential material through Bloom.

### Technical and operational information

Bloom and its hosting, database, storage, and content-delivery providers may process technical information needed to run and secure the service, such as timestamps, error information, request information, and network or server logs. The exact logs and retention periods can depend on the deployment and provider configuration.

## 2. How Bloom uses information

We use information to:

- provide, maintain, and personalize Bloom’s features;
- save profiles, inventories, virtual balances, transactions, reminders, and other requested game state;
- send requested cooldown and reward reminders through Discord;
- measure feature usage and diagnose reliability or performance issues;
- detect abuse, cheating, fraud, unauthorized access, and attempts to manipulate service records;
- enforce these Terms, server rules, and moderation decisions; and
- comply with legal obligations and protect the rights, safety, and security of users, servers, Bloom, and others.

Where required by applicable law, our legal bases may include providing the service you request, our legitimate interests in operating and securing Bloom, your consent where we request it, and compliance with legal obligations.

## 3. How information is shared

We do not sell or rent personal information and do not use Bloom data for behavioral advertising.

Information may be shared with or accessible to:

- Discord, because Bloom operates through Discord and uses Discord’s API and services;
- database, hosting, storage, logging, and content-delivery providers that process information for the operation of Bloom, including configured MongoDB infrastructure and BunnyCDN for card artwork where applicable;
- authorized Bloom staff and server staff who need information to provide support, investigate abuse, moderate a server, or protect the integrity of Bloom; and
- authorities, advisers, or other parties when reasonably necessary to comply with law, enforce our Terms, respond to legal process, or protect people, systems, or property.

When Bloom sends a response to a Discord channel, the response and any information shown in it may be visible to other members of that server. Discord, server owners, administrators, and other users may separately copy, retain, or redistribute information posted in Discord.

## 4. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Bloom, maintain game and transaction integrity, enforce moderation decisions, investigate abuse, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and maintain backups or operational records. There is no single retention period for every category of information.

Monthly usage telemetry, transaction history, moderation records, and other records that support security or economy integrity may remain after you stop using Bloom. Burned-card shop listings are configured to expire after approximately seven days, but that automatic expiry does not necessarily delete related account, inventory, transaction, or moderation records.

In-memory caches and temporary processing data are cleared according to the application’s runtime behavior, expiration settings, or process restarts. Hosting and provider logs may have separate retention periods.

## 5. Your choices and privacy requests

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of, or a copy of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. These rights are subject to legal exceptions and may not apply in every situation.

To make a request, contact us through the [official Bloom support server](https://discord.gg/playbloom). Include your Discord user ID and enough information for us to understand the request. We may need to verify that you control the relevant Discord account. Do not send your password, token, payment credentials, or other authentication secrets.

Bloom does not currently provide a general in-bot data deletion command. Deleting information may remove profiles, inventories, balances, albums, transactions, or other features and may not remove copies already posted in Discord, retained by Discord, held by other users, or required for fraud prevention, moderation, legal compliance, or the integrity of shared records.

## 6. Children

Bloom is not directed to children who are below the minimum age permitted by Discord or applicable law. Do not use Bloom if you are not permitted to use Discord. If you believe a child has provided personal information in violation of applicable law, contact us through the support route above.

## 7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures appropriate to the service to protect information. No online service, Discord account, or database is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee that information will never be accessed, altered, disclosed, or destroyed improperly.

## 8. International processing

Bloom and its service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Those countries may have different data-protection rules. Where required, we will use legally recognized safeguards for international transfers.

## 9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when Bloom, its data practices, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be posted at the location where this Policy is published, with a revised “Last updated” date. If changes are material, we will provide notice where reasonably practical.

## 10. Contact

The operator responsible for Bloom is **the Bloom team**. For privacy questions or requests, use the [official Bloom support server](https://discord.gg/playbloom).
